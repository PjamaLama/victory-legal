Delete your Victory account

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Victory is published by Marnu van Niekerk. You can delete your Victory account and all of its data at any time, in either of these two ways.

Option 1: In the app

  1. Open Victory and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: By email

If you can't open the app, email marnu.v.n@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my Victory account”. Your account and data will be deleted within 30 days, and we will reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. It removes your account and all associated data: your profile, check-ins, journal entries, streaks, and accountability partner pairing.

What is kept

Purchase and subscription records that Google Play is legally required to keep are retained by Google, not by Victory. Cancel any active subscription in the Google Play Store app before or after deleting your account so you are not charged again.

See also the Privacy Policy.
